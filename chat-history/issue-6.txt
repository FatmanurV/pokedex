  ## Goal

  no screen for this, so it follows the style of the detail screen.

  ## Constraints

  that Pokémon is the first one (`/compare?first=25`). Header with "Vorige", as

  - The second one is picked on the same screen with the search field and cards

  - In the picker, tapping a card makes it the second Pokémon (it does not open
  the detail). The cards have no ⋮ there, so there is no options menu on the
  compare screen. `PokemonCard` gets an optional prop to hide the ⋮.

  found".
